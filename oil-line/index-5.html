Seed Oil Sourcing: Margin & Guest Impact

A working model of the cost-versus-value trade behind a systemwide oil switch.

Chipotle has flagged rising oil costs as a margin headwind, and is backing Zero Acre Farms through Cultivate Next to move away from seed oils over time. This model separates the two questions worth asking before that decision: what it costs, and what it's worth.

Supply chain considerations for net margin impact

Sourcing, storage, and logistics cost of a less commoditized oil at this volume — the piece that would move this from a gross to a net margin view.

3

Validate the guest-side numbers

CRM segmentation on existing diet patterns (low-carb, no-dairy, etc.) gives a first read on guests who'd change behavior.

Customer survey on willingness to switch sharpens that same number, and gives an early read on frequency lift.

Pilot marketing test in a real Zero Acre market builds real confidence behind frequency lift, specifically.
